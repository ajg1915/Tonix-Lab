Getting started
Your first stream, step by step
  1. Open Studio and press Power on. Pick a preset and speak: the meters should move.
  2. Open Go live and press Start preview. Allow the camera and microphone when your browser asks.
  3. Pick a Quick preset. Balanced is a safe start for most people.
  4. Open Share & embed, copy your viewer link and send it out.
How streaming works here

When you press Go live, your camera and mic open inside Tonix Lab and are sent straight to your viewers through VDO.Ninja, a free open source streaming service. Its servers only help your device and your viewers find each other. The video itself goes from your device to theirs.

  • Your viewer link opens a Tonix Lab watch page. Your OBS / Streamlabs link goes straight to VDO.Ninja, which is the lightest option for OBS.
  • Every viewer receives their own copy from your device, so a small audience works best. Use a strong connection and keep the page open.
  • Anyone with a link can watch. Do not post a private room name.
Using it on a phone or tablet
  • Use the tab bar at the bottom to move between Home, Studio, Go live, Share and How to.
  • Add it to your home screen for a full-screen app: on iPhone tap Share then Add to Home Screen; on Android tap Install.
  • Keep the screen on while live. Plug the phone in, because streaming drains the battery quickly.
  • Landscape gives a bigger preview. Portrait suits TikTok-style vertical streams.
OBS Studio and Streamlabs
Add your stream to OBS Studio
  1. In Share & embed, copy the OBS / Streamlabs source link.
  2. In OBS, under Sources, press + and choose Browser.
  3. Paste the link into URL. Set Width and Height to match your stream, for example 1280 by 720.
  4. Tick Control audio via OBS so the sound shows up in the OBS mixer.
  5. Press OK, then drag the corners to fit your scene.

To keep OBS light, use the smallest size and frame rate that still looks good, and avoid 60 fps unless the action needs it.

Add your stream to Streamlabs Desktop
  1. Copy the OBS / Streamlabs source link from Share & embed.
  2. In Streamlabs Desktop, open your scene, press + under Sources and choose Browser Source.
  3. Paste the link into URL and match the width and height to your stream.
  4. Press Done, then move and resize it in the scene.
Quality and bitrate
Which bitrate should I use?

Bitrate is how much data per second you send. More looks sharper but needs a faster connection. As a rule, keep the total under 70% of your upload speed.

360p400-1000600-1500
480p600-1500900-2200
720p1500-40002500-6000
1080p3000-65004500-9000

Values are video kbps. Add the audio bitrate on top.

Fixed or Adaptive?

Fixed holds the bitrate you set. It is predictable and best for OBS or a steady connection.

Adaptive starts there and lowers quality when your connection struggles, so viewers keep watching instead of buffering. Pick it on mobile data or shaky Wi-Fi.

Which video encoder?
  • H.264: works almost everywhere and uses your device's hardware encoder. Best default, and the lightest on batteries.
  • VP8: widely supported and simple. Slightly less efficient than H.264.
  • VP9: sharper at the same bitrate, but uses more CPU.
  • AV1: the most efficient, but heavy. Only use it on a recent, powerful device.

Encoders your device lacks are greyed out on the Go live page.

Fix it
Camera or microphone is blocked
  1. Tap the lock or settings icon beside the web address.
  2. Set Camera and Microphone to Allow.
  3. Reload the page and press Start preview again.

On iPhone: if that fails, open Settings, then Safari, then Camera and Microphone, and choose Ask or Allow.

Inside Instagram, TikTok or Facebook: these in-app browsers often block the camera. Use the menu to Open in browser.

No sound, or very quiet
  • Check the Microphone choice on Go live: it may be pointing at the wrong device.
  • Watch the meter under the preview. If it does not move, the browser is not hearing the mic.
  • Make sure the phone is not on silent and no other app is using the mic.
  • With Auto gain off, speak closer or raise the mic level in your device settings.
Echo, feedback or squealing
  • Wear headphones. It is the only sure fix.
  • Turn on Echo cancellation if you must use speakers.
  • Lower the speaker volume and keep the mic away from it.
Bluetooth earbuds sound bad or lag

When a Bluetooth headset's own mic is used, playback drops to phone-call quality and adds delay. Pick a separate microphone as your input and keep the earbuds for listening only. In the Studio, the Align slider fixes voice that lands late against a backing track.

Choppy video or buffering
  • Pick Mobile data or lower the resolution and frame rate.
  • Switch bitrate mode to Adaptive.
  • Move closer to the router, or use 5 GHz Wi-Fi or a cable.
  • Close other apps and tabs that use the camera, upload or lots of CPU.
  • If the device gets hot, drop to 30 fps and choose H.264.
Preview shows a different size than I picked

Browsers treat resolution and frame rate as a request, not an order. The line under the preview shows what you actually got. Many phone cameras cap at 30 fps in low light or on the front camera.
