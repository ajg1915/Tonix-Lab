Quick method. Works fully offline in your browser, but only helps when the singer is exactly in the middle of the mix. It does very little on most real songs, especially live recordings. Use the AI separator for those.

1. Choose a song

This file is mono, so both sides are identical and there is nothing to separate. Vocals cannot be removed from it with this method. Try a stereo version.

2. Choose what to keep

Method

Smart works on each sound separately and copes better with real recordings. Classic is plain left-minus-right subtraction: quick, and clean when the singer is exactly in the middle.

How tightly to aim at the middle
Wide

Lower takes out more, including some of the music. Higher takes out only what is clearly in the middle, which leaves more of the singer behind.

Strength
100%

100% takes out as much as possible. Lower it to leave a little of the singer in.

Keep the bass below
120 Hz

Kick and bass sit in the middle of the mix, just like the vocal. This keeps them.

Keep the highs above
9.0 kHz

Keeps cymbals and hi-hats bright. Raise it if some singing is left, lower it if the music sounds thin.

3. Listen and save

Ready

Working on it. Long songs take a few seconds.

Still hearing the singer? Set the aim to Widest and the strength to 100%. A faint echo of the voice can remain, because the reverb and doubled vocals on a real recording are spread across both sides and no simple method can reach them.

Works best on stereo songs where the singer is in the center, which is most studio recordings. It cannot remove reverb, or backing vocals placed to the sides. The file saves as a WAV, which is large but lossless.
